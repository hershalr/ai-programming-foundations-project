A Cross-Country Database of Fiscal Space
23 Indicators | 1990 - 2022 |202 Economies (Updated: about 1 year ago)

Source: World Bank (WB)(opens in a new tab)

A cross-country database of fiscal space covers 202 countries over the period 1990-2022, and the fiscal space indicators are grouped into four categories:
- Government debt sustainability
- Balance sheet composition
- External and private sector debt
- Market access

For further details, please refer to https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/601211501678994591/pdf/WPS8157.pdf
